Demand forecasting demo
static demo, precomputed data

From a sales forecast to an order quantity

Two years of transactions of a UK online giftware wholesaler (Online Retail II, UCI, CC BY 4.0) as weekly demand. A global LightGBM model forecasts every product 8 weeks ahead with quantiles; the order quantity is a quantile of the demand over lead time plus one week, taken from forecast sample paths (P-quantile), not the forecast itself.

  • Forecast: replay the forecasts of 2011 for a product, with the 60, 80 and 95 % intervals.
  • Policy: simulate the test weeks (June to November 2011) for three order policies; change the service target, margin, holding rate and lead time.
  • Decision: the order list of a week, as a CSV.

This static version shows representative products; the totals of the Policy page cover all top-revenue (A) products.
